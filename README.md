Implementation and experiments for
deep-learning segmentation of catheters and guidewires on the CathAction dataset,
covering U-Net, TransUNet, and Swin-UNet under a unified training pipeline.

The three tests and trains are for the 3 model correspondingly, while evaluate is for validation and preprocess for process data before train.

The directorys hold the composition of our model. Some tools and loss definition are in utils.

I have left 3 traditional training logs for all the three models correspondingly.
